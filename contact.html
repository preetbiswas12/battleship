Send a Message

This form opens your email app with your message pre-filled — no data is stored on this website. You can also write to us directly at quide04@gmail.com.

We only use this to reply to your message. It is never shared.

What to Expect

  • We usually reply within 2–3 business days.
  • Bug reports: tell us what browser and device you were using, and what you clicked before the problem appeared.
  • Advertising enquiries: include "Advertising" in the subject line.
  • Privacy questions: see our Privacy Policy, or mark your message "Privacy".

Before You Write…

  • The AI seems unfair: the computer fires at completely random squares — sometimes it gets lucky streaks. That's part of the classic game!
  • "I lost immediately": each new game places fleets randomly, so outcomes vary. Check the strategy tips to sharpen your aim.
  • A page looks broken: try refreshing (Ctrl+R / Cmd+R) first — and if it still looks wrong, send us a screenshot if you can.
