      Quick Tips

      • Red squares are hits, grey circles are misses. A dark red square means that ship is sunk.
      • Ships never touch diagonally or overlap — every ship occupies a straight line of its exact size.
      • Once you score a hit, the ship must be somewhere in line with that square. Work outward from it.
